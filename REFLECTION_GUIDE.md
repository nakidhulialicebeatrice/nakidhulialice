# Final Reflection — Planning Guide (250–500 words required by assignment)

The assignment specifies that AI may assist with prompting, code generation, database design and architecture, **but not in writing the reflection**. Use these questions to write your own reflection in your own words. Do not submit this guide as the reflection.

Suggested structure:
1. **System and integration (about 60–100 words):** Explain why you selected student management and fees/finance. Describe the single MySQL database, foreign keys, unique identifiers, validation, and derived balance view. Mention what you tested.
2. **Evaluation of AI (about 60–100 words):** Describe the real prompts you used and the output that helped. Give a specific example of a mistake, gap or assumption you discovered—or honestly state if you did not encounter one and explain what you checked. Explain how you verified the code.
3. **Language and scalability (about 50–90 words):** Explain why PHP, PDO, MySQL and XAMPP fit your skills and environment. Discuss what would need to change for many schools or thousands of users.
4. **Managerial / BPR insight (about 60–100 words):** Compare building a custom prototype with configuring a package such as Odoo. Consider training, requirements gathering, maintenance, security, migration, support and process redesign.

Before submitting, make sure your reflection is your own work, falls within 250–500 words, and addresses all four required questions.
